Team Rating History

The Team Rating at every point of every season since 2014: preseason, after each week, and after the bowls and playoff. Point at the line, or tap it, to see that week's game. Click a season to open its games below. Ratings rise at the end of a season because bowl and playoff games carry extra points.

Every game

Game Rating is what the team earned for that game on the 0 to 100 scale. Team Rating is the team's rating once that week's games were counted. The Matchup Rating is the pot the two teams split, and strength is what the opponent added (its Team Rating minus 50). Bowl and playoff games also show their extra points.
