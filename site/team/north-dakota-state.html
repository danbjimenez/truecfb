Game Rating is what the team earned for that game on the 0 to 100 scale. Team Rating is the team's rating once that week's games were counted. The Matchup Rating is the pot the two teams split, and strength is what the opponent added (its Team Rating minus 50). Bowl and playoff games also show their extra points.
